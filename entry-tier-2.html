Entry Models · Tier 2 · Intermediate

Liquidity and trend flow

Two models: the liquidity raid generalized beyond the opening range, and the rung where you stop fighting trend days. Script access unlocks here — the 8BF toolset is earned, not given.

07.1

Model 3 · Sweep & Reclaim

Tier 2 · Intermediate ES + NQ 3m trigger · 1m entry 09:30–11:30 ET liquidity pools

Why it works. Obvious levels grow stops behind them. The market is an engine for finding that liquidity: a spike through PDL fills institutional buys with the stops of trapped longs — then price leaves. The sweep is the liquidity event; the reclaim is proof it was a raid and not a genuine break. This is Model 2's logic generalized from the opening range to every pool on the map — the new skill is recognizing pools and reading reclaim strength.

CardRule
BiasWith the reclaim — away from the swept pool, toward the opposite pool
SetupA visible pool: PDH/PDL, ONH/ONL, session extreme, or equal highs/lows. Price trades through it (the sweep)
TriggerReclaim: a 3m close back on the original side of the level within 3 bars of the sweep. The faster and bigger the reclaim bar, the better
EntryAggressive: reclaim-bar close. Conservative: limit at the level on the retest (fewer fills, better R). Pick one per session and journal it
StopBeyond the sweep extreme + buffer
InvalidationNo reclaim within 3 bars, or a second sweep deeper than the first → the pool is being accepted through, not raided
TargetThe opposite pool (range high for a low sweep). Natural geometry ≈ 2R; sub-1.5R = skip
Management½ at +1R, BE; runner to the opposite pool. Exit runner if price stalls > 15 min at mid-range
Skip whenSweep is driven by a scheduled print (let it settle) · trend day already established through multiple pools · reclaim bar itself is > 1.5R tall (stop too far — wait for retest or pass)
↯
With the tool (Tier 2+): HTF Raids marks sweeps of the higher-timeframe pools so you're never hunting levels mid-session; Fractal Liquidity maps the minor liquidity (equal highs/lows) between them. Watch absorption at the extreme — heavy volume with no downside progress during the sweep is the strongest reclaim precursor there is.

Playing it with our levels

The A+ version: a sweep through a public pool into a GreekZonesPro wall that then reclaims — e.g. PDL swept, the flush dies exactly at the put wall from the daily drop, reclaim follows. The pool supplied the fuel, the wall supplied the floor. With Battle Zones: a sweep through a zone's far edge that reclaims the near edge is the zone-native version of this trade. Ramp-read gate: on strong negative-gamma days, downside sweeps tend to extend rather than reclaim — halve size or demand the retest entry.

Capture spec — v1.1 screenshots
  • 3m ES: PDL sweep → 1-bar reclaim → opposite-pool target hit. Mark pool, sweep wick, reclaim close, both entries, stop.
  • 3m ES: sweep dying on a put wall from the daily drop — annotate wall price + drop text.
  • Counter-example: sweep with no reclaim inside 3 bars → acceptance and continuation lower. Captioned "no reclaim, no trade".
Journal card · Model 3 — Sweep & Reclaim
  • Pool identified and marked before the sweep, not after
  • Sweep actually traded through the level (not just near it)
  • Reclaim close came within 3 bars — otherwise no trade, no exceptions
  • Entry style (reclaim close vs retest) declared for the session and followed
  • Stop beyond the sweep extreme before entry, never widened
  • Target = opposite pool, ≥ 1.5R at entry
07.2

Model 4 · Flow Pullback

Tier 2 · Intermediate NQ primary 5m regime · 1m/3m entry 09:45–11:30 ET VWAP + flow

Why it works. Models 1–3 are counter-trades; this is the rung where students learn to stop fighting trend days. On a real trend day, every pullback to VWAP is institutions reloading at fair price. The model has three independent gates — regime (5m says trend), location (pullback into VWAP/band, not mid-air), flow (participation returns with the trend) — and all three must agree. Most student losses on this model come from trading it on rotation days; the regime gate exists to make that impossible by rule.

CardRule
BiasContinuation — with the established trend only, never counter
SetupRegime gate (5m): price one side of a sloping VWAP with structure agreeing (higher lows for longs) — post-access, the MMB-Elite regime read is this gate. Location: first or second pullback into VWAP / the first band
TriggerPullback stalls at the zone (small bars, wicks holding), then a resumption bar: 1m/3m close back in trend direction with flow flipping with it
EntryClose of the resumption bar
StopBeyond the pullback swing low/high (whichever side the trade is), i.e. just past VWAP
InvalidationClose through VWAP against the trend during the stall → regime is in question, stand down and re-gate
TargetT1 = prior swing extreme · runner = trail behind 3m swings or the band. Trend legs pay 2R+ when the regime is real
Management½ at T1, BE; trail the runner — this is the one model where the runner is the point
Skip whenVWAP flat / price whipping both sides (rotation day) · third+ pullback of the same leg (trend is aging) · pullback comes on expanding counter-flow (that's distribution, not a pullback)
↯
With the tool (Tier 2+): this model's chart is session VWAP + bands with an up/down-volume flow read underneath — the "flow flips back" trigger is literally the flow histogram turning. Market Maker Series 3 on 5m is the regime gate (~30-min windows); Rolling VWAP & Values Lines adds slope-aware VWAP lines if you want the trail rail. Pair the 5m regime with a 15m context chart, nothing exotic.

Playing it with our levels

The level map decides whether the trend has air. A regime-approved pullback entry with the next GreekZonesPro wall 40+ NQ points away is a runner trade; the same entry directly beneath a wall is a scalp to the wall and out. Rule of thumb from the drop: enter at fair price (VWAP), pay yourself at the wall. On positive-gamma pin days the trend gate will simply keep you out — that's the system working, not a missed trade. Battle Zones act as the pullback's backstop: a pullback that holds zone + VWAP confluence is the highest-grade location this model produces.

Capture spec — v1.1 screenshots
  • 1m NQ + session VWAP / flow pane: trend leg, pullback on cooling flow, flow flip + resumption entry. Mark all three gates.
  • 5m NQ with MMB-Elite: the regime read that authorized the session's longs.
  • Counter-example: flat VWAP whip day where the regime gate said no — captioned "no regime, no model".
Journal card · Model 4 — Flow Pullback
  • Regime gate passed on 5m before looking for entries (logged the read)
  • Entry only in trend direction — zero counter-trend entries
  • Pullback reached VWAP / first band — no mid-air entries
  • Waited for the resumption bar with flow agreeing — no knife-catching the pullback
  • Stop beyond the pullback swing, never widened
  • Runner managed by trail rule, not by feelings; third-pullback entries skipped
